# Phase 7 — Data Licensing & Intellectual Property Checklist

**Repository:** GaurviDEEP  
**Working Branch:** `phase7-research`  
**Governing Milestone:** Milestone 4.5 (Gate 1 Data Readiness Specification)  
**Status Date:** 2026-10-09  
**Status:** BINDING LEGAL VERIFICATION PROTOCOL  

---

> [!WARNING]
> **Fundamental Legal Invariant:** A website terms-of-service disclaimer or generic API click-through is **NOT** a substitute for negotiated commercial licensing rights. Before any commercial deployment, paid subscription launch, or external display of model signals generated by GaurviDEEP, every item on this checklist must receive written, executed confirmation from the authorized data provider.

---

## 1. Research, Machine Learning & Model Training Rights

- [ ] **1.1 Internal Quantitative Research Rights:**
  - Written confirmation that GaurviDEEP researchers have unrestricted rights to parse, manipulate, filter, and backtest across the full historical dataset.
- [ ] **1.2 Machine Learning & AI Model Training Rights:**
  - Explicit contractual authorization permitting the ingestion of raw data into machine learning algorithms (Ridge, LightGBM, XGBoost, Ranking models).
  - Confirmation that model weights, feature importance matrices, and trees trained on the data are the exclusive intellectual property of the research sponsor.
- [ ] **1.3 Derived Data Creation & Ownership Rights:**
  - Explicit confirmation that derived mathematical indicators (e.g. forward returns, cross-sectional ranks, residual alpha, volatility scores) constitute "Derived Data" owned 100% by the research sponsor.
  - Confirmation that derived data from which original raw pricing cannot be reverse-engineered is free from vendor redistribution restrictions.

---

## 2. Commercial Application, Display & Access Rights

- [ ] **2.1 Commercial User Access Rights:**
  - Authorization to use model outputs in a commercial SaaS platform or paid advisory service.
- [ ] **2.2 Application Display Rights:**
  - Written permission to display model predictions (e.g. percentile rankings, Top-20 portfolio constituents, sector allocations) in web application dashboards.
- [ ] **2.3 Raw-Data Redistribution Prohibitions:**
  - Clear contractual boundary acknowledging that raw market data (OHLCV, tick, full Bhavcopy) will **never** be redistributed or made directly downloadable by end users.
- [ ] **2.4 API Redistribution Restrictions:**
  - Confirmation that downstream API endpoints (e.g. FastAPI routes in GaurviDEEP) transmitting rankings or portfolio allocations comply with vendor redistribution guidelines.
- [ ] **2.5 User-Count & Seat Restrictions:**
  - Explicit terms defining whether licenses are structured per named researcher, per CPU/server instance, or enterprise-wide.
- [ ] **2.6 Geographic Restrictions:**
  - Confirmation of global hosting and deployment rights without regional usage bans.

---

## 3. Infrastructure, Hosting & Retention Rights

- [ ] **3.1 Cloud-Hosting Authorization:**
  - Written permission to store and process the dataset on commercial cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, Azure, or private VPS).
- [ ] **3.2 Third-Party Processor Rights:**
  - Confirmation that third-party cloud compute providers (e.g. managed database instances, container clusters) are recognized as authorized processing agents.
- [ ] **3.3 Backup & Disaster Recovery Rights:**
  - Explicit permission to maintain encrypted archival backups and redundant failover clusters.
- [ ] **3.4 Data Retention Upon Subscription Termination:**
  - **CRITICAL:** Confirmation of post-termination rights. Does the contract require deletion of the historical training dataset upon contract termination, or is perpetual internal retention permitted for research auditability?
  - Confirmation that derived models and frozen candidate weights may be retained indefinitely regardless of contract termination.
- [ ] **3.5 Termination & Purge Obligations:**
  - Defined protocol for data purging if contract explicitly mandates raw data deletion at contract end.

---

## 4. Reporting, Publication & Audit Compliance

- [ ] **4.1 Publication of Aggregate Research Statistics:**
  - Right to publish summary academic or research statistics (Rank IC, Sharpe ratio, drawdown charts, factor performance) in research papers or model cards.
- [ ] **4.2 Publication of Historical Model Performance:**
  - Authorization to display backtested historical equity curves and out-of-sample live shadow results to prospective clients or investors.
- [ ] **4.3 Use in Regulatory & Client Reporting:**
  - Written confirmation that generated portfolio allocations and risk statistics may be included in compliance reporting or formal investment memorandums.
- [ ] **4.4 Vendor Attribution Requirements:**
  - Specific contractual wording, citation requirements, or copyright notices required on public reports or dashboards (e.g. *"Market data sourced from NSE Data & Analytics"*).
- [ ] **4.5 Delayed-Data Display Requirements:**
  - Clarification of whether end-of-day data display requires a mandatory delay (e.g. 15-minute delay or next-day publication).
- [ ] **4.6 Vendor Branding & Trademark Constraints:**
  - Guidelines governing whether vendor names or index trademarks (e.g. "Nifty 500") may be cited in product positioning without supplemental trademark licenses.
- [ ] **4.7 Vendor Audit Obligations & Verification:**
  - Scope and frequency of vendor compliance audits (e.g. remote architectural review vs on-site inspection).
